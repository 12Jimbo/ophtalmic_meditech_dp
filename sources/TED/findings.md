
TED - [Tenders Electronic Daily](https://ted.europa.eu/en/)
Is a branch of the Publication Office of the European union.
It publishes public procurment data - data about devices purchased by public administrations in Europe.
Tenders = bidders (administration)
Electronic = TED is a supplement to the official journal of the EU, but it's electronic format only (more like eTD)

There are a range of APPs and [APIs](https://docs.ted.europa.eu/api/latest/index.html) for publishers, validators, and analysts.
Analysts are encouraged to participate and use the data: tools are provided and auth is not required to get the data.
